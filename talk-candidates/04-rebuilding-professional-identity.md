# Rebuilding Professional Identity

Working candidate based on the site's original **Rebuilding Professional Identity** path.
The sequence is retained, with the workflow section updated to reflect the intended walkthrough.
These are sections to develop into slides, not individual slides or fixed timings.

**Central question:** If writing code becomes less scarce, what should a software engineer understand their value to be?

**Thesis:** Software engineers have a favorable starting position and must expand from making toward directing, deciding, connecting, and owning outcomes.

## 1. What is actually dying?

Separate the transformation of the role from what happens to the person whose career and identity were built around it.

## 2. My trajectory

Neuroscience → scientific software → infrastructure → AI-assisted full-stack product work.
How my range expanded, and what that has allowed me to do.

## 3. What expansion costs

Lost mastery, shortened comprehension, and cognitive saturation.
Greater capability changes both my relationship to the work and the satisfaction I get from it.

## 4. Four dispositions

Making, directing, deciding, and connecting.
How the balance changes, and what engineers can carry forward or develop.

## 5. A converging role

Knowing which outcome matters and knowing how to produce it dependably.
Engineers and domain experts approach this work from different starting points.

## 6. A walkthrough of how I work now

Code moves into the background; projects, threads, context, and decisions occupy the foreground.
Show the expansion of work I now track and how profoundly this differs from working in an IDE.

## 7. Deliberate reinvention

Reconsider what I want from work as its terms change.
Exercise agency, explore possible directions early, and allow for the loss involved in rebuilding a professional identity.
